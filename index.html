Client Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information regarding our private engagement process, timelines, and commitments.

How does Zora Homes guarantee zero budget overruns?

Before signing our execution contract, Devika conducts an exhaustive structural audit and MEP mapping. We produce a locked-in Bill of Quantities (BOQ). Any discrepancy during construction is absorbed by Zora Homes—not the client.

Can I retain my own architectural framework while hiring Zora Homes for interiors?

Yes. While we excel at turnkey design-builds, Abijith frequently collaborates with external architects to oversee interior master planning, rare stone procurement, custom millwork, and luxury styling.

What is the typical timeframe for a full estate transformation?

Typical residential projects range from 14 to 28 weeks depending on the scale and custom millwork requirements. Phase 01 and 02 design takes 4–6 weeks, followed by on-site execution under strict milestone deadlines.

How is client privacy and NDA protection maintained?

We maintain strict non-disclosure protocols for all projects. Site workers and artisan subcontractors sign binding confidentiality agreements. Project locations and client identities are never publicly disclosed without written consent.
